Safely sharing information
    - Identify sensitive data: 
        + Safeguarding sensitive data is important for protecting its reputation and success
        + Sensitive data contains important information about a business or its stakeholders that, if mishandled, could cause harm or misuse
    - Protect sensitive data:
        + Authentication and authorization
        + Roles and permissions
        + Row-level security (RLS)
        + Data Encryption
        + Data masking
