What is workspace?
    - Overview of workspaces:
        + Use workspace feature to manage data assets in one place, using personal and shared environments
        + these include organization; access control; collaboration; streamlined updates    
            * Organization: Each workspace is a unique container for related reports, dashboards, and datasets => This helps keep your data tidy and easy to locate
            * Access control: safeguard your data, from unauthorized users, with your workspace access control features. =>  Depending on the workspace, you can determine who can see or edit the content
            * Streamlined updates: Updating or modifying data is much easier, with everything in its right place.
    - Types of workspaces: two main types of workspaces
        + Personal workspaces: 
        + Shared workspaces: It's a space designed for collaboration, allowing multiple users to add, edit, and refine reports and dashboards simultaneously. 
    - Best practices:
        + Perform regular clean-ups
        + establish clear naming conventions
        + Review access controls
        + Perform regular backups
        + Encourage a feedback loop

Managing audiences in workspace apps
    - Workspace audiences: a powerful new feature of workspace apps that makes these apps more flexible
    - Advantages of audiences in workspace apps: directly interact with the data pertinent to their roles or departments
    - End-users can view tabs at the top of the screen for each audience they can access.
