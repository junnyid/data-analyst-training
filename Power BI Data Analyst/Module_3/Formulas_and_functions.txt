1. Question 1
In your worksheet, cell A2 contains the word Super. Cell B2 contains the word Cycles. You add the following formula to your worksheet:
=CONCAT(A2," ",B2," ", "Inc.")
What is the result of this formula?
=> Super Cycles Inc.

4. Question 4
True or False: You can display the serial number behind a date by changing the cell format to General.
=> TRUE

5. Question 5
You have typed the date 05/30/23 directly in cell A2  of your worksheet. The date is in the MM/DD/YY format. What date will this cell display when you open the worksheet again two days from now?
=> 05/30/23
(When you enter the date 05/30/23 in MM/DD/YY format, Excel will treat it as a static value. It will not automatically update unless a formula or function is used to modify the date. Therefore, the cell will display 05/30/23 when you reopen the worksheet unless you manually change it or use a formula that increments the date.)
