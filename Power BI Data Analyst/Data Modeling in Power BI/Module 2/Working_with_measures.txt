The CROSSFILTER function
    - to modify the direction of filtering between two tables in a relationship
    - it allows you to override the default relationship direction (one-way or both-way) between two tables, which can be useful when you want to control how filters propagate across relationships in certain situations.
    - Control how filters are applied in both directions (using BOTH), in one direction (ONE), or with no filter propagation (NONE).
    - When to use:
        + Handling Complex Relationships
        + Avoiding Circular Relationships
        + Performance Optimization
