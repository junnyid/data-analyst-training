Data Analysis Process Overview

Prepare Data => Analyze Data => Develop Insights => Build Story

    1. Data Preparation
        Exploration & Preprocessing
        - The data preparation stage focusses on understanding raw data and transforming it into a format that's optimized for analysis.
        - These tasks:
            + Adjusting data types
            + Combining tables
            + Identifying relationships between fields
            + Creating calculated fields
            + Adding filters
            + Reshaping data

    2. Data Analysis
        Visualizations & Statistics
        - There are 03 main steps:
            + Creating visualizations
            + Calculating Statistics
            + Identifying patterns

    3. Insight Development
        come from observations & identified patterns - actionable trends & patterns
        - Ask yourself questions:
            + What are the key takeaways from the data or information?
            + How does this information provide a new perspective or understanding?
            + How does this help me solve the business problem or challenge?

    4. Data Storytelling
        resonates with audience - may need worksheets or dashboards - provides context & interpretation
        - A Tableau story involves arranging worksheets or dashboards into a sequence similar to a slide deck
        - understanding of the data's implications, a clear vision of the key messages 
        - build worksheets or dashboards to successfully convey your narrative


Data Exploration
    - data exploration is about asking the right questions, while preprocessing is about taking the right actions in response to those questions.
    1. Exploring Structure and Metadata
        - The structure refers to the physical layout of your data — how it's organized in terms of rows and columns
        - Metadata, on the other hand, is the information about the data within your dataset, such as field names and data types.
        - Preparing the structure and metadata of a dataset generally involves three tasks: 
            + Determining structure
            + Renaming fields
            + Editing field data types
